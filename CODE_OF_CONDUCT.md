# Code of conduct

Everyone should be able to participate in rules_elide without harassment.
Be respectful, give constructive technical feedback, credit others' work, and
make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas
and code rather than people.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, unwanted sexual attention, sharing
private information without consent, and retaliation against people reporting
concerns are unacceptable. These expectations apply to project issues, reviews,
discussions, and other spaces where participants represent this project.

Report conduct concerns privately to **security@elide.dev**, with the subject
“rules_elide conduct report.” Include relevant context and any immediate safety
concerns; avoid posting private details publicly. Maintainers should protect
reporter privacy, hear relevant perspectives, and avoid handling complaints in
which they have a conflict of interest.

Depending on severity and repeated behavior, maintainers may request a correction,
remove content, warn a participant, or restrict participation temporarily or
permanently. Good-faith reports and respectful disagreement are welcome.
